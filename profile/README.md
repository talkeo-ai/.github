# Talkeo

**The AI English tutor that knows what you know.**

English isn't one skill. Vocabulary, grammar, speaking, listening, reading, writing — you're not the same level in all of them, and no single number describes you. Talkeo measures each area from how you actually use the language — never tests — and is honest about the rest: what it hasn't measured yet, it says so, instead of guessing.

**Under the hood: a map of your English.** 40,000+ words and phrases, each graded by meaning. For every one, Talkeo records whether *you* know it — across **18 dimensions of knowing**: recognizing it spoken or written, producing it, its parts, its meaning, its collocations, its register, and more. The aim is a working copy of your English, in one map. From it, Talkeo reads your level per area, spots exactly what's missing for what you want to do, and picks what to practice next — and every claim carries how sure it is.

Then it gets personal. The map isn't only what you know — Talkeo also learns who you are: your goals and what's at stake in them, your needs, your interests, the situations you're actually walking into. Knowledge on one side, *you* on the other — and that's what lets it calibrate the perfect session: every minute of practice is exactly the thing you need next, in content from your world, not from a generic syllabus.

And you can see it happen: your level per area, what's still unmeasured, and how far you've moved since you started.
